conninfpy.interpret

Anatomical interpretation of inference results with the Brainnetome atlas (BNA-246; Fan et al., 2016). Everything here describes a result: nothing adds a test, and the p-values, their correction and the inference unit (edge, component or block) are unchanged. The rules (tissue overlap, purity, network labels, declared template) and worked calls are in Anatomical interpretation — conninfpy.interpret.

  • annotate_rois() describes the ROIs of any atlas or coordinate set by the BNA regions their sphere, parcel or point covers; annotate_bna_regions() annotates data parcellated with BNA-246 itself.

  • annotate_edges() (also annotated_edges()) and summarize_annotation() describe the connections against the tested opportunities. aggregate_connections() sums them over Brainnetome units and reports atlas coverage in attrs['coverage']: unassigned ROIs (outside the atlas or below min_coverage) are not placed, and the detected connections touching them stay counted.

  • interpret_result() does all of it in one call and returns an InterpretationReport (tables, evidence packet, connectograms, Markdown section).

Interpretation of inference results: Brainnetome annotation, reports and evidence.

  • conninfpy.interpret.annotate: ROI and connection annotation with the Brainnetome atlas (annotate_rois(), annotate_bna_regions(), summarize_annotation(), aggregate_connections());

  • conninfpy.interpret.report: one-call interpretation of a result (interpret_result()), tables, evidence packet and connectograms;

  • conninfpy.interpret.evidence: the evidence packet built from the annotation;

  • conninfpy.interpret.motion_qc: additive, opt-in descriptive motion (framewise displacement) and QC-FC side-car diagnostics computed before inference (motion_qc_fc(), motion_qc_fc_paired(), resolve_fd()), with a Markdown section (motion_qc_markdown()) for reports.

conninfpy.interpret.annotate_rois(coords: ArrayLike | None = None, *, reference: BrainnetomeReference | None = None, names: Sequence[str] | None = None, space: str = 'MNI152NLin6Asym', support: str = 'sphere', radius_mm: float | Sequence[float] | None = None, parcel_image: Any = None, parcel_labels: Sequence[int] | None = None, purity: float = 0.5, min_coverage: float = 0.5, nearest_mm: float | None = None, min_weight: float = 0.2, networks: Sequence[str] | None = None) → RoiAnnotation[source]

Describe each ROI by the Brainnetome regions its signal came from.

Parameters:

  • coords (array of shape (n_rois, 3), optional) – ROI centres in mm, in connectivity-matrix order. Required for support='point' and 'sphere'; for 'parcel' the parcel centroids are used when omitted.

  • reference (BrainnetomeReference, optional) – Loaded with BrainnetomeReference.load() when omitted.

  • names (sequence of str, optional) – ROI names (default ROI_001 …).

  • space (str) – Template of the coordinates. 'MNI152NLin6Asym' (FSL MNI152, the atlas’s own grid) is exact; 'MNI152', 'MNI152NLin2009cAsym' (fMRIPrep) and similar MNI variants differ from that grid by an unquantified amount and are flagged approximate. The default assumes FSL MNI152 and adds no flag, so set space whenever the signals were extracted in another template (fMRIPrep’s default output is 'MNI152NLin2009cAsym'; 'MNI152' when the variant is unknown). Talairach coordinates must be converted first (e.g. Lancaster et al. 2007); the space is never guessed from values.

  • support ({'sphere', 'parcel', 'point'}) – What produced each ROI’s signal: a sphere of radius_mm around the centre, the voxels of parcel_labels in parcel_image, or (when nothing else is known) the centre point alone, flagged as a point estimate.

  • radius_mm (float or sequence of float) – Sphere radius (per ROI or one for all).

  • parcel_image (nibabel image or path, optional) – Integer label image the ROI signals were extracted with (MNI).

  • parcel_labels (sequence of int, optional) – Label value of each ROI in parcel_image (matrix order).

  • purity (float, default 0.5) – A level (subregion, gyrus, lobe; network) is assigned when its largest share of the labelled support is at least purity.

  • min_coverage (float, default 0.5) – Least labelled share of the support for any assignment; below it the ROI is ‘unassigned’ (white matter, CSF, cerebellum, brainstem or outside the brain: BNA-246 covers cerebral cortex and subcortical nuclei only).

  • nearest_mm (float, optional) – Point support only: an unlabelled centre takes the nearest labelled voxel within this distance, flagged with the distance. Off by default.

  • min_weight (float, default 0.2) – Regions holding at least this share of the labelled support contribute their BrainMap profiles.

  • networks (sequence of str, optional) – The source atlas’s own network label for each ROI (e.g. the Yeo-7 label of each Schaefer parcel). It becomes the network column used for network relations, network grouping and connectogram colours. When omitted, the Brainnetome Yeo-7 division of the ROI’s tissue is used (yeo7: assigned under the same purity rule, else ‘mixed’). For data parcellated with the Brainnetome atlas itself use annotate_bna_regions().

Returns:

table columns: roi, x, y, z, support, radius_mm, n_voxels, coverage, level, assigned, region, region_purity, gyrus, lobe, hemisphere, structure, description, yeo7, yeo7_purity, yeo17, yeo17_purity, regions, membership, domains, profile_status, flags, network. yeo7 and yeo17 each follow the purity rule on their own shares (cortex only).

Return type:

RoiAnnotation

Annotate data parcellated with the Brainnetome atlas (BNA-246) itself.

Each ROI is exactly its BNA region: the support is the region in the atlas’s own label image, so it is assigned at subregion level with purity 1, and its gyrus, lobe, hemisphere, description and BrainMap profiles come from the atlas. Network labels come from the Brainnetome division table (bna246_hierarchy.csv; division='yeo7' or 'yeo17'), not from a spatial overlap; subcortical regions, which the division leaves unassigned, are labelled ‘Subcortical’.

Parameters:

  • names (sequence of str, optional) – ROI names (default: the BNA region names).

  • division ({'yeo7', 'yeo17'}) – Column of the Brainnetome division used for the network column.

conninfpy.interpret.annotate_edges(edges: Any, rois: RoiAnnotation, *, smoothing_fwhm_mm: float | None = None) → Any[source]

Add endpoint anatomy and endpoint relations to an edge table.

edges needs roi_i / roi_j (matrix indices), e.g. from result.significant_edges(...); its columns (statistics, p-values) are kept unchanged. Added columns:

  • i_roi / j_roi (ROI names), i_bna / j_bna (assignment at the finest pure level), i_lobe, j_lobe, i_network, j_network (the network column), i_yeo7, j_yeo7 (Brainnetome crosswalk), i_domains, j_domains;

  • hemispheric_relation: left / right intrahemispheric, homotopic (homologous subregions), homotopic gyrus, heterotopic, or bilateral/midline endpoint;

  • anatomical_relation: same subregion, same gyrus, same lobe, between lobes (hemisphere ignored; see the hemispheric relation), or mixed / unassigned;

  • circuit: cortico-cortical, thalamo-cortical, cortico-basal ganglia, amygdalo-cortical, hippocampo-cortical, or the subcortical pair;

  • distance_mm between the ROI centres;

  • shared_support: share of the smaller sphere shared with the other (> 0 means overlapping spheres: the edge partly reflects the same voxels, not coupling between regions), and with smoothing_fwhm_mm within_smoothing: centres closer than r_i + r_j + FWHM;

  • sc: support-weighted population structural connectivity (NaN when the tractography maps are unavailable; low values do not show absence of a pathway);

  • network_relation: within <network> / between / n/a, from the network column (the source atlas’s division, see annotate_rois());

  • shared_domains: BrainMap domains present at both endpoints (descriptive context, not the function of the connection).

conninfpy.interpret.summarize_annotation(result: Any, rois: RoiAnnotation, *, alpha: float = 0.05, tail: str = 'both', param_idx: int | None = None, level: str = 'lobe', distance_bands: Sequence[float] = (30.0, 60.0, 90.0), smoothing_fwhm_mm: float | None = None) → Dict[str, Any][source]

Describe where the detected connections lie, against what was tested.

Every table puts the detections next to the tested opportunities, because composition alone misleads when categories differ in size (there are far more cortico-cortical than thalamo-cortical pairs). Nothing is tested: no enrichment p-values, and the original correction and inference unit (result.inference_unit: edge, component or block) are unchanged.

Returns a dict of DataFrames:

  • relations: by circuit, hemispheric relation, anatomical relation, distance band, network relation and (when the result carries coefficient maps and a comparison) coupling change: n_tested, n_detected, share of detections, share of tested, detection rate;

  • regions: per group at level: n_rois, tested and detected incident connections, detection rate (ROIs not pure at that level are grouped as ‘mixed’);

  • domains: per BrainMap domain, unique ROIs among detected endpoints vs among all ROIs (memberships overlap: shares need not sum to 1);

  • distance_qc: Spearman correlation of the signed and absolute statistic with distance over all tested connections, and the share of detections vs tested connections shorter than the first band. A contrast that covaries with head motion often shows short-range increases and long-range decreases (Power et al. 2012).

meta['roi_restriction'] records which inference unit the ROI tables describe, the counts they are built from, and the names of the unassigned and mixed ROIs: those stay their own group and are never folded into a total.

conninfpy.interpret.aggregate_connections(result: Any, rois: RoiAnnotation, *, level: str = 'gyrus', tail: str = 'positive', alpha: float = 0.05, assign: str = 'weighted', networks: Sequence[str] | None = None, param_idx: int | None = None, nonzero_only: bool = True) → Any[source]

Detected and tested connections summed over Brainnetome units, split by network.

A connection between ROIs i and j adds w_i[a] * w_j[b] to the unit pair (a, b), where w is each ROI’s share of labelled support per unit (assign='weighted') or 1 for the unit holding most of it ('dominant'). With weighted assignment one connection contributes 1 in total, so detected reads as an expected number of connections. Each contribution is also labelled by the network of each endpoint ROI (networks, default rois.table['network']), giving one row per (unit_a, network_a, unit_b, network_b). tested is the same sum over every tested connection, so rate = detected / tested compares unit pairs sampled by different numbers of ROIs.

ROIs that annotate_rois marked level == 'unassigned' (no labelled support, or a labelled share below min_coverage) are not placed: their weight rows are zero, so their connections carry no rows here. The returned frame carries df.attrs['coverage'] with the atlas coverage of the aggregation: n_rois, n_placed, unplaced_no_support and unplaced_low_coverage (ROI names), unplaced_rois (both lists), detected_total and detected_placed (detected connections of tail with finite p — all, vs. those with both endpoints placed; counted once with tail='both'), tested_total / tested_placed, inference_unit (the unit of the result whose member connections are counted: edge, component or block), and level, assign, tail. Use it to report placement alongside the table; conninfpy.plot.connectograms.plot_connectogram() puts it in its footer and defaults to assign='dominant' while this function defaults to the fractional 'weighted'.

Descriptive only: the result’s correction and inference unit are unchanged.

Descriptive motion / QC-FC diagnostics for paired conditions A and B.

Each condition gets its own diagnostic with FD tied to its own rows (one observation per subject and condition). ids_group1/ids_group2 verify the strict A<->B ID bijection and move condition B and its FD vector into condition A’s order before anything is summarised; without them the rows are taken positionally. Pooling across conditions happens only with that verified identity and is reported as stacked observations (a subject contributes one row per condition, so the two rows are not independent). The condition difference is deliberately not computed: a B - A descriptive difference must not be mixed into the common FC QC.

Extra keyword arguments go to motion_qc_fc() (scale, pheno, coords, atlas, space, nuisance, …).

Evidence packet

Evidence packets for interpretation text, built from Brainnetome annotation.

The packet holds only what the analysis and the atlas support:

  • query - the contrast, the source atlas, the detected connections and their inference unit (edge, component or block), plus roi_restriction: which unit the ROI counts belong to, the counts they are built from and the names of the unassigned and mixed ROIs;

  • reference - Brainnetome atlas and BrainMap provenance, ROI support;

  • rois - anatomy of every endpoint ROI (assignment level, region, lobe, hemisphere, structure, cytoarchitectonic description, Yeo network, BrainMap domains, flags) and how many detected connections touch it;

  • terms - BrainMap terms of the BNA regions each endpoint covers, with the published forward-inference likelihood ratio (never combined across regions or endpoints);

  • relations - optional detection counts by circuit class, hemispheric relation and distance band, next to the tested opportunities;

  • caveats.

No term is ranked as “the” function of a region, and nothing here is a test.
