Brainnetome Atlas data in this directory
========================================

The following files are Brainnetome Atlas data or derived from it, and are NOT
covered by ConnInfPy's MIT licence (which applies to the code only):

  BN_Atlas_246_1mm.nii.gz, BN_Atlas_246_2mm.nii.gz   maximum-probability label images
  bna246.csv                                        region names and voxel-mass centroids
  bna246_hierarchy.csv                              hierarchy built from BNA_subregions.xlsx
                                                    (Fan et al. 2016, Table 1) and
                                                    subregion_func_network_Yeo_updated.csv
  BDf_FDR05.json, PCf_FDR05.json                    BrainMap behavioral-domain and
                                                    paradigm-class profiles of each region
                                                    (forward-inference likelihood ratios,
                                                    FDR q < 0.05), as published on
                                                    atlas.brainnetome.org

Source: Brainnetome Center, Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences,
https://atlas.brainnetome.org (download page: /download.html).
