Beijing EOEC (China) Open-Close derivatives (not bundled in repository)
=======================================================================

The Beijing Eyes-Open / Eyes-Closed (Beijing EOEC) derivative files are NOT
bundled in this repository because of their Creative Commons
Attribution-NonCommercial (CC BY-NC) licence:

  china_open_Schaefer200_strategy-4_GSR.npy    Schaefer-200 eyes-open time series
  china_close_Schaefer200_strategy-4_GSR.npy   Schaefer-200 eyes-closed time series
  subject_order_china.txt                      subject IDs aligned to row index

How to obtain and prepare locally:
  1. Download the preprocessed derivatives archive from Medvedeva et al. (2026):
     https://disk.yandex.ru/d/fNz33QPwJQj7HQ
     (Original cohort source: https://fcon_1000.projects.nitrc.org/indi/retro/BeijingEOEC.html)
  2. Run the preparation script to verify shapes, alignment, and SHA-256 checksums:
     python scripts/prepare_china_open_close.py --source-dir /path/to/downloaded_dir
